### 🔨 Building now: [Hyto](https://github.com/Hyto-App/hyto)

Expense control and milestone payments for teams and events. The budget sits in a **Trustless Work multi-release escrow on
Stellar testnet**; a member uploads photo evidence, an **AI review** (Llama 4 Scout on Groq, plus Laya) recommends, and an
admin approves each USDC release.

- Built with a team of five for Stellar's **Find Your Way** hackathon (Costa Rica, Sep 2026) — I own the **admin experience**
  and the [Hyto-App](https://github.com/Hyto-App) org.
- Stack: Next.js 16 · Vercel · Cavos wallet · Neon Postgres · Vercel Blob · Groq
- Status: testnet / hackathon build.
